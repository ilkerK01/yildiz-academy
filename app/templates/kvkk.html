Collection method and legal grounds

Your data is collected electronically, partly by automated means, through the sign-up form, the settings page and your use of the platform. The legal grounds under Article 5(2) of KVKK are:

  • Necessity for the establishment and performance of the membership contract (c): account, progress, points, ranking, profile photo, password reset
  • Legitimate interest of the data controller (f): information security and abuse prevention
  • Compliance with a legal obligation (ç): requests from competent authorities and statutory retention
  • Establishment, exercise or protection of a right (e): possible disputes

Who can see your data

Your nickname, profile photo, points, ranking and completed labs are visible to other members. Your e-mail address is never shown to other members.

Transfers

Your personal data is not sold or shared for advertising. It is transferred only in the following cases:

  • To hosting and infrastructure providers, to the extent needed to run the platform
  • To the e-mail delivery provider, limited to your e-mail address and the message content, for password reset e-mails
  • To competent public authorities, where required by law

The servers of the e-mail delivery provider may be located abroad. Transfers abroad are carried out in line with Article 9 of KVKK.

Fonts on the pages are loaded from Google Fonts. While doing so, your browser sends technical connection data, including your IP address, directly to Google servers.

Retention

Your data is kept for as long as your account remains open. If you want your account deleted, you can request it as described under How to apply; your account and the progress records linked to it will be deleted. Session records become invalid when they expire, and password reset records become invalid once used or expired. Records that must be kept by law are retained for the required period.

Your rights (Article 11 of KVKK)

  • To learn whether your personal data is processed
  • To request information if it has been processed
  • To learn the purpose of processing and whether it is used for that purpose
  • To know the third parties in Türkiye or abroad to whom it is transferred
  • To request correction if it is incomplete or inaccurate
  • To request deletion or destruction under the conditions in Article 7 of KVKK
  • To request that correction and deletion be notified to third parties to whom the data was transferred
  • To object to a result against you arising solely from automated analysis
  • To claim compensation if you suffer damage due to unlawful processing
